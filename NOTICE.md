## Onshape's icons

The keys show Onshape's own toolbar icons, but none of them are in this
repository or in the plugin package. The Chrome extension reads them out of the
Onshape page the user is signed in to, and the plugin caches them in its data
folder (`~/Library/Application Support/Logi/LogiPluginService/PluginData/Onshape/icons`).
They remain PTC's.

The one place they do appear is `docs/keypad.png`, a screenshot of Logi Options+
showing the keypad as the plugin draws it.

## Trademark

`OnshapePlugin/src/package/metadata/Icon256x256.png` is the Onshape logo, taken
from the app Chrome creates for cad.onshape.com and cropped to fill its canvas.
